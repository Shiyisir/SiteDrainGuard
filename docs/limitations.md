# Limitations

- The bundled site, rainfalls, facilities and costs are synthetic.
- The model is not calibrated against field observations.
- No survey DEM, local design-storm generator or live monitoring is included.
- Cost values are user-supplied demonstration assumptions, not a cost database.
- Rational Method is a quantity sanity check, not proof of model correctness.
- Continuity gates are internal project thresholds, not universal standards.
- Risk levels are display heuristics, not regulatory classifications.
- v0.1 runs one process with sequential SWMM simulations and does not promise production multi-user concurrency.
- Arbitrary user INP files, GIS/DEM, LID, optimization and code-compliance judgements are out of scope.
